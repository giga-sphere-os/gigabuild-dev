GigaBuild data request path

To delete GigaBuild account or configurator data, email the privacy team from the address connected to your order or account.

Email deletion request

What To Include

Include your name, company, order ID if known, and requested workspace domain so the team can locate the right record.

What Happens Next

Giga-Sphere will verify the requester, delete eligible account/configurator data, and confirm completion. Some records may be retained when required for payment, tax, legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.
